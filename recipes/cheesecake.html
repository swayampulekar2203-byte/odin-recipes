Recipe for Oreo Cheesecake

This Oreo cheesecake is easy to prepare and a great dessert to bring to your next get-together.

Ingredients:

You'll need just six ingredients to make this easy Oreo cheesecake recipe:

  • Oreos: 24 Oreo Cookies, divided. You'll need about 24 Oreos (16 for the crust and eight for the filling).

  • Butter: 3 tablespoons butter, melted. Melted butter gives the crust moisture and helps bind the cookie crumbs together.

  • Cream Cheese: 3 (8 ounce) packages Philadelphia Cream Cheese, softened. The Oreo cheesecake filling starts with three packages of cream cheese.

  • Sugar: ¾ cup sugar. White sugar is beaten with the cream cheese for the sweet filling.

  • Vanilla: 1 teaspoon vanilla. Vanilla extract enhances the flavor of the decadent cheesecake.

  • Eggs: 3 large eggs. Whole eggs lend moisture and help bind the cheesecake batter together.

How long does Oreo cheesecake last?

Store the cheesecake in an airtight container or wrapped in aluminum foil in the refrigerator for up to five days.

Can you freeze Oreo cheesecake?

Yes! You can freeze Oreo cheesecake for up to three months. We recommend freezing the cheesecake in individual slices so you don't have to thaw the whole thing every time you want a piece.

Directions:

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).

  2. Place 16 cookies in a resealable plastic bag. Flatten bag to remove excess air, then seal bag. Finely crush cookies by rolling a rolling pin across the bag.

  3. Place crushed cookies in a bowl. Add melted butter; mix well.

  4. Press mixture firmly onto bottom of a 9-inch springform pan.

  5. Beat cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla in a large bowl with an electric mixer on medium speed until well-blended.

  6. Beat in eggs, 1 at a time, until just blended.

  7. Chop remaining 8 cookies; gently stir 1/2 of the chopped cookies into cream cheese batter.

  8. Pour over prepared crust; sprinkle remaining chopped cookies on top.

  9. Bake in the preheated oven until center is just set, about 45 minutes.

  10. Refrigerate for 3 hours to overnight. Cut cheesecake into 12 equal pieces; store leftovers in the refrigerator.
